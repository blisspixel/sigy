# Strange signals and curious investigations

Updated: 2026-10-03. Status: confirmed later product direction; the workflows below are proposals. No claim-extraction engine, IQ/astronomy importer, SETI detector or autonomous investigation is implemented. [Primary-source research](../../research/41-strange-signals-and-claim-exploration.md) records sources, alternatives and qualification limits. This extends [Intent](../../INTENT.md) without changing the first-release dependency order.

## The experience

Open Sigy to follow an intriguing statement, hear the original, compare accounts and investigate a strange signal. People interested in subjects discussed on Coast to Coast AM, Joe Rogan or other public shows can use rigorous tools without giving up curiosity or a sense of humor. These examples express audience interests raised by the user; they establish no affiliation or assumptions about listeners.

Make the desk immersive and enjoyable: a generous timeline, exact clip comparison, source and language lanes, a later labeled waterfall and saved investigation context. Optional playful names or commentary can give a session character. Factual status, failures, gaps and scientific labels retain plain wording. The same useful operations remain available through CLI and bounded structured agent access.

Keep four kinds of material visible: observed data, an attributed claim, a proposed explanation and an unknown. A model can suggest a relation or next check under finite authority. It cannot certify truth, invent observations, change budgets or silently widen a watch.

## First: compare exact passages

Begin with two to eight existing retained citations. Read the original wording beside a qualified chosen-target translation, inspect neighboring context and available playback, then save a user-attributed relation such as quoted source, possible paraphrase, explicit correction or disagreement. Preserve recording identity, cue times, transcript/translation revisions, uncertainty and retention status.

Recording position, episode publication time, asserted event time and local observation/import time are separate coordinates. Missing or disputed dates retain their source and precision. Each timeline lane names its time basis; a confident-looking chronological display cannot manufacture compatible clocks.

Repeated script, the same press release or the same witness can explain several broadcasts. Similar wording suggests a lead, with no proof of dependence or collusion. Different wording gives no proof of independence. Claims of independent observation remain attributed claims until their provenance is checked. An initial comparison can work without a truth score, universal fact classifier, live search agent or new graph database.

Report the earliest passage found in the declared searched library, sources and time window. Preserve examined versus available material, language coverage, truncation, gaps and missing recordings. An empty literal search has a limited retrieval meaning. Suggested contradictions display both passages and the differing referent, date, translation or assumption. Corrections append history and identify affected interpretations.

Acceptance uses independently specified examples of quotation, negation, sarcasm, changed dates, translated wording, shared source and explicit correction, with unsupported relations left unresolved. Human annotations and model proposals remain distinct. Schema/replay correctness and semantic relation quality are separate gates.

## Later: replay a strange signal

A SETI watch begins with one explicitly selected public archival observation and the product actually available. Reduced spectra do not preserve every property of raw voltage/IQ. Imported data retains upstream identity/version/license, complete or partial hash/range, numerical representation, channels, time scale, sample rate, frequency reference, tuning epochs, gaps and available calibration. Missing required metadata holds a dependent transform while byte inspection remains possible.

Every waterfall or derived measurement records transform version/parameters, window/normalization, resolution, masks and display reduction. Axes, units and a legend remain readable in the terminal and plain output. Uncalibrated power stays relative or arbitrary; unavailable physical units and sky coordinates remain unavailable. Resampling or reprocessing the same recording is another calculation over one observation.

Start with maintained format specifications, independent numeric fixtures and one licensed bounded archive product. Select Rust implementations intentionally after comparing correctness and deployment costs. A source file reader, detector, telescope profile and actual follow-up each need their own evidence. No bulk observatory mirror or unattended multi-gigabyte download is implied by opening this desk.

## Finally: a finite watch

Explicitly choose accepted sources/products, monitored ranges, cadence, duration, byte/CPU/storage caps, alert rules and the maximum planned effects. Use the canonical scheduler, jobs, grants, exact ledger, retention and recovery. Persist useful partial observations across client exit. A detector result can propose a next observation; service policy decides whether its finite authority permits it.

Interference/reference comparisons preserve different observation durations, sensitivity, noise floors, timing and instrument assumptions. A missing reference or failed test remains inconclusive. Declared alert thresholds need measured false-alert behavior on an appropriate labeled population; a ranking score is not a calibrated probability of extraterrestrial origin. Published BLC1 analysis is a required interference counterexample for the design.

Physical redetection requires another actual observation with its own provenance and appropriate source/instrument checks. A saved checklist helps reproduce an investigation and does not turn its completed boxes into scientific proof. Live receive-only hardware follows qualified adapters and actual device evidence. This path remains useful even when the investigation finds a mundane explanation.

## Build placement

The first cited-comparison slice follows dependable recording/playback, revision inspection and bounded interpretation storage. Public archive replay follows typed file/signal transforms and reference fixtures; live watches follow qualified reception and resource admission. Initial work is local with zero metered inference. Optional providers and agent planning retain explicit destination/capability qualification and finite limits.
