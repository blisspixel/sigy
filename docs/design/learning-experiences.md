# Optional learning experiences

Status: confirmed intent, proposed design. The 2026-10-03 followup confirms an optional playable campaign using actual tool components, history beyond the United States, accessible hints and deeper engineering/math. C-50, R-69, D-44 and E-34 identify its planning followup. Nothing here claims implemented lessons, approved assets, qualified languages or cryptographic support. [Research43](../../research/43-historical-and-modern-learning.md) records primary sources, current standards and item limitations.

## A real instrument with an optional route through it

The campaign composes library inspection, typed transformations, bounded jobs, evidence and explanation. Each action is an actual operation with an inspectable outcome, not a pretend terminal or a parallel game backend. Opening a lesson grants no network, device, capture, retention, processing or paid authority. A badge, hint, score or completion unlocks navigation only; authorization remains service-owned.

Keep ordinary exploration available without the campaign. Resume a lesson through immutable artifact references and a bounded attempt record; never replay mutations blindly or refill an allowance. Capture and analysis continue independently of rendering. Existing [terminal](terminal-experience.md), [task](task-workflows.md), [signal-extension](../planning/08-signal-extensions-and-workbench.md) and [Strange Signals](strange-signals.md) contracts remain authoritative.

Country-media lessons use the same explorer and recording/citation navigation as ordinary listening. Historical transforms and modern crypto extend the typed workbench later. Their presence in a learning map does not make missing capabilities usable: unavailable, empty, unknown, partial, interrupted and refused outcomes are first-class states, with a useful explanation and return path.

## Play and presentation

Offer Observe, Try, Hint, Reveal and Explain with reversible controls and an untimed default. A concise introduction presents one object, one question and one useful action. Optional expert panes expose procedure, arithmetic, trace, assumptions and measured work. Hints release one declared clue at a time and record assistance; they never secretly repair an input or fabricate a success.

Children and newcomers get readable text, modest vocabulary, an explain-first path and optional sound. Advanced users can inspect exact artifacts, compare candidates and work through deeper mathematics. Avoid pressure timers, public ranking, ridicule, combat spectacle and distressing testimony in the default introductory route. Do not turn a living language into a score-based guessing game or claim a child solved a language by matching a lookup table.

An optional 1943-inspired desk uses original modern layout and restrained period atmosphere, labeled modern reconstruction. Actual artifact date, source, authored additions and absent original audio remain visible. It does not imply historical terminals existed in that form or that synthetic Morse/speech is an original intercept. Theme, reduced motion, monochrome, linear access, keyboard navigation and compact 80x24 operation preserve the same facts as the wide view. Inspect representative rendered screens; layout tests alone do not qualify usability.

## Modern discoveries and playful challenges

Modern experiences are a prominent campaign route: inspect the user's own permitted weather-sensor reports or LoRa packet trail, replay bounded 1090 aircraft frames and distinguish current from expired positions, compare an ISS pass prediction with an actual observation, or inspect a new GOES weather image after its antenna/accessory/decoder gates are met. [Receiver research42](../../research/42-calm-listening-and-receive-only-sources.md#useful-bounded-receiver-experiences) supplies current primary sources and availability limits. Offline fixtures and actual device qualification remain distinct; a predicted pass or announced event does not guarantee reception. These adapters and lesson paths remain unimplemented.

Signed-message tampering, post-quantum KEM plus reviewed AEAD, separate signature verification and synthetic noise/detection trials supply equally modern local routes without hardware. No fictional quantum-break meter or visual padlock substitutes for the actual operation. An owned-device example does not authorize inspecting someone else's packets, changing radio settings or transmitting.

Illustrative workshop labels include Dial Explorer for inspecting a declared source selection, Night Shift for a completed finite overnight observation, Signal Sleuth for testing competing explanations on one artifact, and CQ Curious for an appropriate public amateur-signal learning exercise. These are tentative names, not selected badges or operator endorsement. An achievement binds actual typed receipts and their complete/partial outcome, input identities, assistance and limits. It never grants new operators, processing allowance, budgets or device/network permissions. Interesting legal activities depend on the actual source and jurisdiction; no global permission is inferred from a label.

A daily donut challenge names specific finite public source revisions associated with a cached country selection, exact UTC or explicitly resolved civil window, selected original/translation revision and versioned literal terms. It may sample bounded clips during a day; it must not imply continuous full-day coverage from those clips or open an unbounded watch. Report scheduled versus retained/processed duration, gaps, failed/unknown language, unqualified text, stale translation and the exact counted citations. Deduplicate repeated matches by frozen original cue identity/revision; choose one revision per cue in the manifest so corrections or multiple translations do not inflate the tally. Counting unique matched cues is distinct from counting word occurrences within a cue; declare which measure is used.

English donut/donuts are explicit terms, not an automatic multilingual concept. Other spellings, language-tagged aliases and matching rules need an independently reviewed dictionary revision; never invent synonyms from a model. Results are literal counts in the selected stored representation, not audio-verified meaning, nationwide mention frequency or general cultural insight. Zero is exhaustive only for complete coverage of that declared representation and scope; incomplete coverage reports observed count plus partial/unknown total, never absence. Negative audio-meaning claims require separately qualified evidence. The campaign can suggest a new bounded investigation while preserving the user's authority decision and all consumed allowances.

## Artifact and answer boundaries

A proposed lesson manifest binds lesson/version, source/item identifiers, dates and uncertainty, hashes, rights/notices, raw transcription, normalization, input type, transform/profile version, independent expected witness, hint schedule and finite work limits. Interpretation and translation are separate derivatives. Updating a lesson creates a revision; it does not rewrite earlier attempts or historical source text.

In challenge mode, keep solution, secret settings, revealing generation seed and answer-bearing traces outside solver inputs, model context, accessible artifacts and logs. Fix the attempt before reveal/compare; record which cribs or hints were released and when. Historical heuristic rankings are not probabilities. An exhausted search cannot prove no solution exists. Learning mode may expose settings openly and must clearly distinguish that assistance from an unknown-key challenge.

Demonstration keys have a distinct lifecycle and cannot silently become operational keys. Normal modern-crypto views show public fingerprints and status, not secret intermediate values. A disclosed demonstration key explains key disclosure, not quantum cryptanalysis. Public content and signed text never grant permissions; a successful tag or signature does not make a message's assertions true.

Synthetic signal manifests pin generator algorithm, seed policy, arithmetic, quantization, sample format, sample clock and serialized hashes. Use independent reference vectors and separate training/test trials; the oracle must not call the production transform. Truth labels cannot be rewritten after evaluation. IQ remains IQ; any demodulated audio is a derived artifact with exact ancestry, never arbitrary samples renamed as a recording.

## Engineering and uncertainty that can be explored

Teach detection, decoding, steganography, cryptography and traffic privacy separately. A known waveform can aid recovery and detection; a public spreading sequence is not encryption. A noise-like display is not proof of secrecy, and encrypted payloads still expose some timing/length/context. Unknown unfamiliar-language content remains unknown.

The optional math pane states model assumptions, units, integration bandwidth and estimator. Shannon capacity is an ideal channel result, not measured decoder performance or security. Compare fixed-power and fixed-energy integration; report false alarms, misses, base rates, search multiplicity and held-out uncertainty. Label dBFS and synthetic values; do not convert them into calibrated dBm or field strength. Never promise undetectability.

Modern lessons use maintained implementations and independently specified final-standard vectors after selection. KEM, key derivation, AEAD and signatures are separate operations. Verify implicit-rejection behavior, nonce/sequence lifecycle, authentication failure, malformed encodings and atomic plaintext publication. Ordinary computers can execute post-quantum algorithms; a lesson does not demonstrate a quantum attack, universal resistance or a validated module. No homemade modern primitive, automatic downgrade or attacks on third-party protected communications.

Country comparisons state cached/observed scope and missing coverage. Preserve station UUID, directory generation, immutable source revision, recording identity/hash and exact cue lineage. Directory geography is not studio, RF transmitter, speaker nationality or CDN origin. Language labels and unqualified transcripts/translations are evidence with limitations, not ground truth. Classification stays off; no ideology, sentiment or country-belief score is inferred. Clicks and cache health do not establish nationwide popularity or representativeness.

## Item-specific release gates

Public availability is not permission to bundle a scan, interview, pronunciation sample or recreation. Before shipping each item, record complete provenance, applicable license/access conditions, permitted excerpt/use, required notices, corrections and derivative labeling. Copyright, community cultural-access conditions and source reliability are separate questions. Preserve third-party legal attribution. An unresolved item stays linked or unavailable while an expressly authored alternative remains separately labeled; do not require a generic approval ceremony for every ordinary exercise.

Candidate archive scans/audio remain unselected. No facsimile or voice is bundled by this design, and no historical setting is accepted merely because a fluent result looks plausible. Verify source-specific vocabulary and spellings without inventing grammar or pronunciation. The campaign creates no institutional affiliation, endorsement, newly arranged reviewer qualification or measured language quality.

## Bounded execution and acceptance

Select limits through the existing job/resource contracts before dispatch: input/metadata bytes, symbols/samples, artifacts, attempts, candidate alignments, output, memory, CPU, deadline and persisted history. Use checked arithmetic and cap allocation before growth. Keep read-only preview separate from transform admission. Cancellation preserves originals and protection until completion is proven; reopen preserves exact receipts and cannot re-execute an effect through a cosmetic replay control.

| Increment | Necessary evidence before enabling it |
| --- | --- |
| Composition and manifest | Keyboard journey, exact identity/return state, unavailable/partial states, malicious markup and terminal text, bounds, immutable revision/reopen, no incidental network/device/spending |
| One historical transform | Independent known answer and stepping boundaries, alphabet conversion, false crib, ambiguous settings, hidden-answer isolation, exhaustion and exact replay |
| One modern profile | Final vectors and interoperability, wrong keys/context/tags, implicit rejection, malformed encodings, nonce/restart failure, secret redaction, measured resources and atomic output |
| One offline signal lesson | Independent integer/numerical witnesses, noise controls, mismatched timing/reference, held-out false alarms/misses, truncation and work exhaustion, sample-gap lineage and disclosed units |

Short timing runs cannot prove constant-time behavior or side-channel immunity. A round trip alone qualifies neither historical nor modern correctness. A successful synthetic trial qualifies no RF reception, live LoRa interoperability or language understanding. Offline replay means analysis or speakers; RF replay and transmission remain outside this learning scope.
