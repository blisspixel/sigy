This remains a staged acquisition plan, not a decoded corpus. The first [Arabic train archive and selected original](arabic-audio-pilot.md) are verified; fifteen archives remain unopened.

Recheck physical free space and task-owned usage at every admission.

The alternative [Parquet range path](parquet-acquisition-review.md) needs 17,200,600,678 bytes of audio chunks and did not offer useful row-level selectivity. The filter has no revision parameter, may return several recordings per sentence ID, uses expiring signed cached assets, and had an index-loading error. It has no publisher audio checksum for each selected row. The original archives provide stronger immutable source identity and a complete train/test route under the new ceiling. The filter remains a metadata cross-check, not the chosen download path.
