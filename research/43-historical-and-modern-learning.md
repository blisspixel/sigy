## Enigma engineering profiles and independent witnesses

Reviewed 2026-10-03. Select an explicit dated machine/profile, not a generic Enigma switch. [Crypto Museum's inspected machine wiring tables](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/wiring.htm) distinguish Army/Air Force Enigma I, naval M3 and M4. Pin ETW, every rotor permutation/inverse, reflector, allowed wheel pool and turnover convention; the keyboard's physical layout is not an ETW permutation. I-V have pre-step window turnover letters Q/E/V/J/Z; naval VI-VIII each have M and Z. The table's physical-notch and turnover columns are different coordinate conventions, not interchangeable values.

[The M4 construction](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/m4/index.htm) adds a stationary Beta or Gamma wheel beside three moving service rotors and a thin reflector. The Greek wheel is not an arbitrary fourth moving rotor. Thin b/c are not ordinary B/C: Beta plus thin b, and Gamma plus thin c, reproduce their corresponding ordinary reflectors at zero effective Greek offset, for example window A and ring A. Compatibility must be tested as a permutation identity, then with complete messages; another Greek setting must not be silently reset. M3's naval wheel pool includes VI-VIII; an Army I-V profile must not silently admit them. Early reflector A is a separate historical profile, not the default wartime B.

[Machine mechanics](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/working.htm) establish movement before electrical contact. For these pawl profiles, evaluate fast/middle notch predicates on the pre-keypress windows, then simultaneously step fast always, middle if fast or middle is at a notch, and slow if middle is at a notch. Do not implement cascading odometer carry. Notches are attached to the index ring: changing Ringstellung changes wiring relative to the displayed letter, not the notch's displayed turnover letter. Other Enigma families with geared stepping require separate rules.

Own mathematical convention: encode A=0 through Z=25, store a rotor's right-to-left permutation f, displayed window p and ring offset r. With d=(p-r) mod 26, forward mapping is f(x+d)-d mod 26; reverse uses f inverse with the same offset. Validate permutations before inversion. If T is the complete forward ETW/rotor mapping at the stepped position, P the pairwise plugboard and U a fixed-point-free involutive reflector, then C=P composed with T inverse, U, T and P is an involution with no fixed points. Check C(C(x))=x and C(x)!=x for all letters at frozen positions. These derived structural properties do not independently validate wiring or stepping; full-message reciprocity requires reset to the identical initial state.

[Hamer's original mechanical analysis](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/people/hamer/files/double_stepping.pdf) supplies an independent III-II-I window trace: ADO, ADP, ADQ, AER, BFS, BFT, BFU. Preserve left-to-right display order despite differing internal rotor numbering. Independently declared transition cases must cover both naval notches, simultaneous fast/middle notch engagement, non-A rings, and an unchanged Greek wheel. Do not generate expected stepping with the production stepper.

The [1930 instructional-message witness](https://www.cryptocellar.org/enigma/e-message-1930.html), identifying translated manual HW25/9, supplies reflector A, order II-I-III, rings 24/13/22, daily window 06/15/12 and six plug pairs. Its repeated encrypted indicator yields message start ABL; the reset body prefix GCDSE yields FEIND. Use the complete document's independent 90-letter witness as well as this short prefix. The reflector-A wiring was recovered through later research, so record that derivation separately from the original instructional text. This is not a wartime live intercept or blanket licensing of the transcription.

For a later-procedure witness, [8 July 1941 message 23 and its reconstructed key](https://cryptocellar.org/bgac/e-keys-jun-oct-1941.html) distinguish the clear indicator, encrypted message key and excluded discriminant; the [original M4 project result](https://www.bytereef.org/m4-project-first-break.html) supplies a different long-message/settings witness. Pin the exact corresponding artifact, plaintext, recovered settings, procedure and rights before importing either. [The procedure timeline](https://cryptocellar.org/bgac/timeline.html) documents changed Army indicators and explicitly distinguishes naval procedures; do not reuse repeated-key or discriminant rules for every message. [Original patent records](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/patents/index.htm) explain historical constructions, but an early patent alone does not specify a later military variant's wiring and procedure.

Qualification needs independent short answers, mechanical traces and authentic long witnesses together, plus malformed settings, wrong-key/false-crib cases, reset/replay and exact trace/output agreement. Public availability is not vector redistribution permission. A round trip or agreement between two programs sharing the same faulty assumptions is insufficient. Search rankings are heuristics, not proof of plaintext or original settings; equivalent settings, transcription gaps, modern recovered keys and historically supplied keys remain distinct. No profile or lesson is implemented or qualified by this review.

## Living languages and code talking

The [NMAI code-talking chapter](https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360/code-talkers/code-talking/) distinguishes ordinary language-mediated messages from specially developed code vocabularies. Its Comanche audio example is explicitly a recreation. Knowing a language and knowing agreed military terms are different capabilities. Diné Bizaad is a living language, not a cipher: the [Navajo Nation's language order](https://opvp.navajo-nsn.gov/executive-order-no-01-2025-dine-bizaad-official-language-of-the-navajo-nation/) supplies present-day community context.

The [Navy's historical account](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/n/code-talkers.html) describes the Navajo wartime code as not broken by Japan. This is an attributed historical account, not mathematical unbreakability or proof that every speaker could decode it. The [1945 published dictionary](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/n/navajo-code-talker-dictionary.html) is not a modern pronunciation course. The claim that the code was cracked but the language remained unintelligible is unsupported by the reviewed sources.

Compare distinct contexts through the [Choctaw Nation's WWI account](https://www.choctawnation.com/about/history/code-talkers/), NMAI's Comanche account and [Veterans Affairs Canada's Cree account](https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/people-and-stories/indigenous-veterans?wbdisable=true). Do not merge varieties, codebooks, participant counts or transmission procedures. An authored lookup exercise must retain exact documented entries and unknown tokens; no invented pronunciation, synthetic veteran voice or claimed language qualification.

The [LOC guide](https://guides.loc.gov/navajo-code-talkers) points to later first-person interviews, not an authenticated wartime enemy-intercept corpus. This review found no complete item combining original intercept audio, coded transcript, matched plaintext and release-ready rights. That is a search limit, not proof none exists. Some Navy/Marine/LOC item fetches were unavailable in the research pass; indexed excerpts cannot qualify adoption of a complete artifact.

[LOC VHP rights](https://www.loc.gov/collections/veterans-history-project-collection/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/), [Smithsonian terms](https://www.si.edu/termsofuse) and [NMAI cultural-access policy](https://americanindian.si.edu/collections-statement) require item-specific review. Public access, copyright status and community access conditions are separate. Preserve required legal notices; claim no endorsement or newly arranged review. Default children's material excludes graphic testimony and combat rewards.

## Hidden in noise and hidden in plain sight

Physical detection, symbol recovery, steganography, cryptography and traffic privacy answer different questions. [NIST SP 800-86](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/86/final) covers embedded information; [RFC 6973](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6973.html) explains that timing, lengths and other traffic characteristics can reveal information despite content protection. A public spreading sequence or obscure encoding is not a secret key.

[Shannon's original theorem17](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf) gives ideal AWGN capacity `C = B*log2(1+S/N)`. Powers refer to the same bandwidth; asymptotic suitable coding, Gaussian noise and channel assumptions are not finite-packet performance, secrecy or semantic understanding. Derived example: 125 kHz and S/N = 0.01 yield about 1794 bit/s, not a LoRa benchmark. [Original AWGN covert-communication research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.6423) establishes a square-root limit under its observer/channel assumptions, not universal undetectability.

Own reference derivation: for independent real Gaussian noise of variance `sigma2` and `y_i=A*b*c_i+w_i`, known sign chips give `z=sum(c_i*y_i)/N`, mean `A*b` and variance `sigma2/N`. Increasing N at fixed chip amplitude/duration spends more symbol time and energy. Fixed-total-energy comparison provides no free AWGN energy gain; synchronization and reference mismatch matter. [Semtech's PHY description](https://www.semtech.com/lora/what-is-lora) identifies LoRa chirp spread spectrum, distinct from this sign-chip model. Its [FAQ](https://www.semtech.com/design-support/faq/faq-lora) describes sensitivity versus symbol-duration tradeoffs; synthetic chirps do not establish full LoRa decoding.

Own detection example: prevalence 0.0001, sensitivity 0.9 and false-positive rate 0.01 give only about 0.89 percent true events among alarms. Freeze thresholds before held-out trials, report misses and false alarms, and disclose search multiplicity. Under independent tests only, at least one false-alarm probability is `1-(1-alpha)^m`. Noise estimation, overlapping windows and real unknown prevalence preclude unexplained certainty scores.

Use `10*log10` for power ratios; `20*log10` for amplitudes requires comparable square-law conditions. FFT window, normalization and resolution bandwidth change displayed noise. Synthetic units and uncalibrated receiver dBFS are not antenna-port dBm. Say not detected by a specified test over specified trials, never undetectable.

## Adoption evidence

Each item needs complete provenance, exact permitted-use scope, source hashes/revisions, independent expected outputs and explicit missing information before distribution. A page-wide license cannot overrule narrower item restrictions. Link-first investigation or clearly authored examples can proceed while particular scans/audio remain unavailable; avoid silently substituting a reconstruction for an inaccessible original.

Independent historical stepping/known-answer witnesses, final-standard [ACVP vector revisions](https://github.com/usnistgov/ACVP-Server/releases), wrong-key/tag/context cases, hidden-answer isolation and deterministic signal reference vectors must precede executable lessons. Seeds alone do not specify a reproducible artifact: pin generator, arithmetic, quantization, format and bytes. Exact work bounds and interruption outcomes require measurement; no asset or capacity qualification follows from this research.

Country-media exploration uses [Radio Browser's documented directory fields](https://docs.radio-browser.info/) as claims about cached entries. Directory geography, RF transmitter, studio, speaker nationality and CDN origin remain distinct. Directory participation, stale health, listening time and language quality bias observed coverage. The lesson preserves exact source/recording/cue provenance and classification-off behavior; a few clips cannot establish a country's beliefs.

Modern sensor/LoRa trails, aircraft replay, ISS prediction-versus-observation and GOES images use the dated primary cases in [receiver research42](42-calm-listening-and-receive-only-sources.md#useful-bounded-receiver-experiences), with its accessory, protocol and live-availability gates. They are contemporary learning candidates, not already qualified adapters. A playful daily literal-term challenge needs finite named public sources, exact time/representation, deduplicated original cues, versioned language terms and explicit gaps. Its count is not a national statistic or verified spoken meaning. Achievements describe actual receipts and confer no authority.
