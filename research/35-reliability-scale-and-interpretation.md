# Reliability, scale and interpretation research

Reviewed: 2026-10-02. Status: primary-source synthesis and repository review. Six perspectives cover durable workflows, native containment, multilingual qualification, physical instrumentation, analytical research and artistic interaction. This review made no inference requests, downloads of models or new recordings, paid allocations, or operational qualification claims. Recommendations are inferences for the [implementation plan](../docs/development/reliability-and-scale.md), not upstream guarantees transferred to Sigy.

The user confirmed exceptional engineering for personal machines and servers, an architecture capable of growing to large deployments, translation beyond English, and openness to unfamiliar or hypothetical representations. Existing source ownership, privacy, exact accounting, local-first defaults and release gates remain in force.

## Durable workflows and scalable authority

[Kubernetes controllers](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/architecture/controller/) reconcile observed state against desired state. [Temporal cancellation](https://docs.temporal.io/develop/typescript/workflows/cancellation) distinguishes requesting cancellation from waiting for completion. These reinforce Sigy's existing bounded service reconcilers and supervision; adopting either runtime would introduce competing workflow ownership without demonstrated need.

Recommendation: freeze exact task collection-to-evidence lineage in one transaction, explicitly admit publication from that digest, and commit each effect with its receipt. Withdraw task interests through append-only history; preserve other owners and wait for actual worker quiescence before releasing capacity. Current direct interests cannot distinguish independent direct callers. Current evidence matching can collect cue rows before the citation cap; independent scan, byte, allocation and deadline bounds are necessary.

[SQLite WAL](https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html) has one writer and same-host coordination; long readers can impede checkpoints. [Synchronous settings](https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_synchronous) distinguish consistency from durability across power loss. [SQLite testing](https://sqlite.org/testing.html) uses crash and I/O fault injection, including recovery failures. Recommendation: keep short catalog transactions, preserve selected durability settings, measure growth and commit tails, and test the media/catalog protocol under compound faults.

[PostgreSQL SELECT](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-select.html) documents `SKIP LOCKED` for queue-like consumers while warning that it produces an inconsistent view. PostgreSQL is a future transactional backend candidate when measured write or ownership requirements justify it. An additional execution worker alone is not that requirement. Brokers can notify but cannot replace Sigy's generation, receipt and ledger authority.

[Overload handling](https://sre.google/sre-book/handling-overload/) emphasizes actual resource cost and graceful restriction. [Bounded Tokio channels](https://tokio.rs/tokio/tutorial/channels) require explicit concurrency and queue bounds. Inference: use aggregate host/device admission, preserve capture/control headroom, show backlog and starvation, and defer analysis before saturation. Slots and average recognition pace alone cannot qualify capacity.

Remote fencing rejects stale results but does not prove remote execution stopped. Network partitions may retain running work or paid liability. Warm workers require a new per-request completion/isolation contract before replacing today's empty-group publication rule. Batching, memoization and speech skipping need quality, privacy and failure measurements as well as speed measurements.

## Native containment and devices

[Windows creation-time job assignment](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20230209-00/?p=107812) and the [process attribute API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/processthreadsapi/nf-processthreadsapi-updateprocthreadattribute) address the gap between process creation and later job assignment. Seek a maintained safe interface; preserve the repository's first-party unsafe-code prohibition. A compiler upgrade alone is not a containment decision. [CPU-rate limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_cpu_rate_control_information) impose maximum rates, not reserved available cores. Job Objects do not deny network access.

[Kernel cgroup v2](https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/latest/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html) supports delegated hierarchies, resource limits, whole-leaf kill and population observations. [systemd delegation](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/main/docs/CGROUP_DELEGATION.md) explains manager ownership and subgroup topology; [kill policy](https://www.freedesktop.org/software/systemd/man/latest/systemd.kill.html) governs service termination. [PSI](https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/latest/accounting/psi.html) measures resource contention. Require real manager owner-death evidence in addition to container runs. Same-UID delegated workers that can modify limits or migrate are bounded cooperative execution, not a hostile-code sandbox.

Read-only review of `day/linux-containment` at `bea26fd4abe122124899aa5031ebc9acc5438ad4` found cleanup on next startup without an external manager, kernel-task rather than exact process-count bounds, and same-UID hierarchy access; its worktree also contains an uncommitted fixture change outside that commit. These remain visible integration limitations. Review of `day/gpu-recognition` at `69b7f08742ee3c0ddb7551e314bdcc9ea562f880` found per-run device evidence, timing under 77 to 100% CPU contention, and Vulkan `small` failure in 31 of 35 runs. Those results cannot qualify capacity or general Vulkan support.

[The upstream recognizer benchmark](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/blob/master/examples/bench/README.md) isolates encoder work using random audio; complete pipeline measurements must also include decoding, VAD, loading and real speech. [Vulkan memory budgets](https://docs.vulkan.org/refpages/latest/refpages/source/VK_EXT_memory_budget.html) are implementation-dependent estimates, not enforceable VRAM quotas. Qualify exact runtime/model/device/driver profiles and admit GPU work across recognition and translation together.

## Translation directions and independent quality

[BCP 47 language tags](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5646), [language matching](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4647), and [Unicode locale identifiers](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr35/#Canonical_Unicode_Locale_Identifiers) distinguish processing identities from presentation negotiation. Preserve script and regional distinctions. Freeze target and canonicalization at admission; a presentation fallback cannot authorize translation into a different target.

The current translation schema has a target constrained to `en`, an `english` cue field and target-free job identity. Target-aware jobs, directed profile capabilities, grants, latest selection and provenance need a schema/IPC migration. Historical English IDs and revisions remain readable. English is a default and current evaluation direction, not an upper bound on useful translation.

[ACES](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01153) supplies translation accuracy challenge methodology. [WMT 2025 adequacy/fluency analysis](https://aclanthology.org/2025.wmt-1.16/) examines evaluator tradeoffs. [Fleurs-SLU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06117) adds downstream understanding tasks but does not establish radio fidelity. Recommendation: evaluate ASR, reference-text translation, ASR-output translation and downstream retrieval separately. Preserve missing outcomes in usable-coverage denominators. Gate severe factual errors, wrong targets and unsupported directions separately from aggregate text metrics. Freeze acceptance before holdout and resample independent speaker/document groups rather than repeated versions of a few sentences.

[The 2025 Québec French benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21193v1) reports that common benchmarks poorly predict its spontaneous-speech conditions. Its corpus access was unresolved at publication. It motivates a distinct Canadian French evaluation without implying rights to acquire or distribute that corpus, or qualification of all Canadian varieties. Navajo speech/reference availability remains unresolved. [Klingon lexical data](https://github.com/De7vID/klingon-assistant-data) is a candidate requiring commit, license and per-entry provenance review; lexicon tests do not qualify speech recognition. Canadian French, Navajo and Klingon remain substantive requirements.

[Non-speech recognition hallucination research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11378) supports testing voice-free material independently. VAD and suppression are transforms with missed-speech risks. Use silence, music, weak/cropped speech, overlap, code switching, boundary phrases and genuine phrases resembling recurrent hallucinations. A phrase blacklist or fluent output does not establish fidelity. No failed local judge can qualify semantic support without new independent calibration.

## Physical instrumentation and unknown signals

[FFmpeg resampling](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-resampler.html) exposes choices affecting delay and output. [Sampling and aliasing](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/technical-articles/2022/07/16/09/20/basics-of-bandlimited-sampling-and-aliasing.html) links sample rate with anti-alias filtering. [SigMF](https://sigmf.org/) and its [schema](https://github.com/sigmf/SigMF/blob/main/sigmf-schema.json) provide an interchange candidate for typed samples and capture metadata; pin a revision before adoption.

Recommendation: record a measurement model with sample format/rate/channel, clock epochs, valid sample-to-media/UTC mappings, transform identity and known uncertainty. Receive time is not emission time. Dimensionless digital amplitudes are not RF power or sound-pressure measurements. Missing samples, retained silence, VAD-skipped material and unrecognized speech are different outcomes. Spectra require window, normalization, interval, bin and unit definitions.

[NIST uncertainty guidance](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297) separates repeat-observation and other uncertainty contributions; [traceability policy](https://www.nist.gov/calibrations/traceability) concerns documented measurement results rather than software branding. [Receiver sampling guidance](https://hackrf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/sampling_rate.html) and [clock interfaces](https://hackrf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/external_clock_interface.html) motivate actual device qualification rather than transferring electrical assumptions between models. Hardware remains later, receive-only, and requires real rate, disconnect, gain, clock and overload evidence.

Independent impulses, ramps, tones and known sample counts can check transform behavior; retained recordings and actual devices establish operational behavior. Test unknown patterns against instrument artifacts, sampling changes and competing hypotheses before discussing meaning or origin. Hypothetical non-human material receives no invented translation or discovery claim.

## Analytical research and social context

[Public analytic standards](https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf) separate source information, assumptions, judgments, contrary evidence and uncertainty. The [Berkeley Protocol](https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/projects/developing-the-berkeley-protocol-on-digital-open-source-investigations/) separates collection, preservation, verification and analysis. These are useful methodology sources, not affiliation, certification or adoption of an institutional mandate.

[AAPOR disclosure standards](https://aapor.org/standards-and-ethics/disclosure-standards/) include content analysis and disclosure of the frame, units, inclusion/exclusion and collection conditions. [Cross-lingual stability research](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.346/) motivates task- and language-specific evaluation, not language-agnostic model claims. [Privacy lifecycle guidance](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) supports reviewing derived projections and sharing as well as raw acquisition.

Recommendation: distinguish observation, what a source alleges, interpretation and hypothesis. Retain contrary evidence and discriminating questions. Track rebroadcast, syndication, quotation and unresolved dependence with evidence; different outlets are not automatically independent origins. Freeze selection scope and missingness denominators. Directory samples and literal absence cannot establish population opinion or event absence. Protect original-script retrieval and minimize unnecessary identifying information in requested exports.

## Artistic attention, poetry and humor

[Listening practice](https://www.deeplistening.rpi.edu/deep-listening/) emphasizes focused and expansive attention. Creator essays on [relative pitch](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/articles/1668521/poets-on-translation-relative-pitch) and [translation choices](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/articles/1685754/poets-on-translation-otherwise-the-same) explore meaning, sound, tone and metaphor. These artistic references do not qualify a model or authorize generated text to replace evidence.

[Accessible exhibition design](https://labs.cooperhewitt.org/2018/accessible-exhibition-guide-delivering-content-to-all/) presents equivalent interpretation through different routes. [Original simplicity principles](https://maeda.pm/2017/12/24/laws-of-simplicity-as-originally-typeset/) motivate calm organization and progressive detail. Recommendation: make wonder arise from real places, scripts, times and audible passages. Offer equivalent text paths, preserve original/target comparison, and separate faithful translation from optional expressive interpretation. View selection grants no work authority.

[Astronomical sonification](https://chandra.harvard.edu/sound/) documents mappings of measured data, and its [participant study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18082) examines access and engagement. [Humorous assistive interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16634) is contextual research with small qualitative samples. Later sonification must name mappings, units and gaps and stay distinct from received audio. Humor belongs in explicitly selected exploration or practice, with locale-specific evaluation; operational failures, spending controls, sensitive material and machine protocols remain literal.

Artistic hypotheses require usability and accessibility evidence: completion, mistakes, comprehension, fatigue and rendering/resource cost. They inform the existing terminal contract rather than delaying core delivery with a new mandatory feature set.

## Explorer, place search and directory recovery

[Ratatui layout guidance](https://www.ratatui.rs/concepts/layout/) supports deliberate flexible composition; the pinned library remains selected. [Animation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) motivates disabling nonessential motion without losing information. [CLI Guidelines](https://clig.dev/) supply command ergonomics to compare against actual parser/help/output behavior. These references do not establish Sigy's terminal latency or accessibility qualification.

[Radio Browser's API](https://docs.radio-browser.info/) provides country codes, free-text subdivisions, optional coordinates, UUIDs and upstream checks, but no canonical city search. Its upstream health is not local playability, resolved URLs are not authority, and clicks are not worldwide popularity. [GeoNames exports](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt) are a candidate for bounded offline place identity and multilingual aliases under CC BY 4.0, requiring attribution, hash/version and accuracy limitations. [Public geocoding policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) prohibits autocomplete and systematic querying; it is not appropriate assumed background infrastructure.

The subsequent worldwide-search clarification requires place choices independent of station rows. [Unicode territory display names](https://cldr.unicode.org/translation/displaynames/countryregion-territory-names) distinguish country/territory identities, localized names and macroregions; evaluate a reviewed current identity set with localized fallbacks rather than using every display entry as a radio country code. Pin the [CLDR release](https://cldr.unicode.org/index/downloads) and preserve its [license](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt) if adopted. No naming asset is selected or imported by this review.

GeoNames documents `cities15000` as places above 15,000 population or capitals, and supplies language-tagged alternate names. Its [feature codes](https://www.geonames.org/export/codes.html) distinguish capitals and government seats from historical places. Evaluate that compact global subset with current capital/government-seat supplementation, then a broader city extract only if measured import/index costs justify it. This is a candidate inclusion rule, not a claim of current population accuracy or every city worldwide. Generate a per-country/territory manifest of included IDs, hashes/dates, missing capitals/aliases and rejected rows; test all accepted places in an empty station library. Keep country selection available where city data is absent.

Recommendation: improve the shared query, stable name ordering and freshness first, then include a complete selected offline country reference before claiming worldwide country navigation. Evaluate disambiguated multilingual city/place lookup with explicit approximate radius matching over directory coordinates. Preserve unlocated stations and useful country/text fallbacks. Extend service-owned refresh only through finite saved bounds, partial-generation accounting and retained identity. Add separately authorized local fetch/decode validation and immutable endpoint repair; browsing and directory metadata cannot grant new stream contact. Inspect wide/compact/offline/error/recovery views and measure actual terminal response under simultaneous capture and processing.

The late-1980s/WarGames visual reference is confirmed product intent. [Ratatui's widget primitives](https://ratatui.rs/concepts/widgets/) support composed tables, canvases, charts and stateful navigation in the existing stack; no dependency upgrade is selected. Favor generous geography, precise time rulers, readable original scripts and restrained phosphor accents. Validate whether the result helps complete real tasks rather than inferring usability from a cinematic reference.

Read-only source inspection found a concrete DVR dependency: the terminal effects have no capture/playback actions, the recording view consumes metadata only, and CLI playback reads one published segment. The service's live action parks at a published end. Planned DVR delivery therefore needs bounded media read leases/next-segment navigation, continuous client playback with codec/gap handling and finite latest-sealed follow, followed by the interactive timeline/cue/finding desk. These are source-derived implementation gaps, not capabilities supplied by a layout library. The [engineering plan](../docs/development/reliability-and-scale.md#explorer-increments) records DV-01 and DV-02.
