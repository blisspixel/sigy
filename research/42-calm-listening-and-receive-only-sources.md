## Practical listening now

The user wants an enjoyable place to listen around the world and later investigate SDR and LoRa sources. Existing [source and recording contracts](../docs/design/recording-metadata.md), [terminal experience](../docs/design/terminal-experience.md#calm-listening-sessions) and [explorer/DVR plan](../docs/planning/11-radio-explorer-and-dvr.md) already establish the shared architecture. Calm presentation means less compulsory information and dependable control, not weaker accounting or invented activity.

Current CLI listening plays one retained published segment or explicitly listens to a direct audio revision. The terminal recording timeline and service playhead sessions are metadata views; they do not yet control audible pause/seek. The next player must bind a protected reader, contained decoder and actual client audio destination before claiming interactive DVR. Selection, source inspection and cached paging remain acquisition-free.

Proposed first session: one foreground audible source, independent inspected source, optional original/target context and a stable return to the same query. Admit each playback explicitly. Replacement stops and proves completion of the old generation before admitting another. Retain one pending replacement, finite execution/output/reader bounds and no automatic retry sequence. Background recordings retain their independent service ownership. Closing the client stops its player without stopping durable collection. Restoring view state never restarts sound.

## Three different observations

| Observation | Useful presentation | Required lineage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IQ or real samples | Spectrum, tuning and sample inspection | Format, rate, sample origin, tuning/configuration epochs, gaps and measured clock mapping |
| Demodulated audio | Player, waveform and later speech/translation | Exact sample parents, demodulator profile, filtering/resampling alignment, uncertainty and propagated gaps |
| LoRa frames or node packets | Packet/event inspector, typed telemetry and text | Protocol/interface version, packet/payload identity, integrity/authentication status, distinct device and local receipt clocks |

These are proposed Sigy contracts. IQ is not audio; a node packet is not a raw RF recording. Binary telemetry does not require speech recognition. A text message can use later text translation under the same target and authority rules. Unknown protocols and inaccessible payloads remain useful observations without invented meaning. English is the default target; interface locale, source language and chosen target remain independent, and actual language quality remains unqualified.

## Primary hardware and protocol findings

[HackRF Pro documentation](https://hackrf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/hackrf_pro.html), reviewed 2026-10-03, describes a half-duplex transceiver, a 100 kHz to 6 GHz operating range and 8-bit I and Q at up to 20 million complex samples/s. Operating range and configurable tuning range differ. These are manufacturer capabilities, not Sigy compatibility or usable-bandwidth measurements. At the stated 8-bit mode, hypothetical raw storage is `20,000,000 samples/s * 2 bytes/sample = 40,000,000 bytes/s`; ten seconds needs 400 MB before metadata and copies. An IQ grant therefore needs its own finite bytes and scratch accounting. Do not generalize that arithmetic to packed or extended-precision modes.

[Current gateware documentation](https://hackrf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/gateware.html), reviewed 2026-10-03, distinguishes standard, half-precision and extended-precision configurations. Container width, effective resolution, USB packing and output rate need separate profile fields; a 16-bit container does not prove 16 effective bits. Qualify firmware, host library, gateware and sample format together. The pinned [Pro r1.2.0 MCU schematic](https://github.com/greatscottgadgets/hackrf-pro/blob/a5f825d7d5fcb2433abd9784dff9deb3e1790d2e/mcu.kicad_sch) places normally bridged solder jumper JP1 on TX_EN. Hardware transmit disable is a physical configuration requiring actual revision inspection, not a software toggle or a modification performed here.

Keep antenna, filter/attenuation chain, gain and clipping in the acquisition receipt. More gain can increase overload rather than useful sensitivity. The [manufacturer's RF-port power specification](https://greatscottgadgets.com/hackrf/pro/) is 3.3 V at 50 mA; leave it disabled unless a selected accessory profile requires it. Do not transfer One's input limits onto Pro. The [ANT500](https://greatscottgadgets.com/ant500/) covers 75 MHz to 1 GHz according to its manufacturer, so it is not a qualified shortwave antenna. Different HF, aircraft and satellite experiences need appropriate independently assessed front ends; no accessory is selected here.

[SigMF's specification](https://sigmf.org/), reviewed 2026-10-03, defines sample representation and dataset metadata with capture segments and annotations. Keep sample-rate/frequency units and capture sample starts explicit. Evaluate a version-pinned projection and independent interoperability fixtures while preserving Sigy's authority, retention and derivative ancestry. Optional interchange fields do not satisfy a transform's required acquisition metadata merely because a file validates. Alternatives are a first-party typed import with a later SigMF projection, or SigMF import first; neither justifies another catalog or a control-channel stream of high-rate samples.

[Meshtastic's client API](https://meshtastic.org/docs/development/device/client-api/), reviewed 2026-10-03, documents serial, TCP and BLE interfaces carrying `ToRadio`/`FromRadio` protocol buffers. Its streaming framing uses a length-prefixed header and documents rejecting lengths above 512 bytes. The page contains both older `startConfig` examples and a `want_config_id` description: pin compatible firmware/protobuf definitions and test the actual handshake before implementation. Packet framing does not bound the complete initial node database, reconnect backlog, debug output or decoded allocation. Set independent aggregate byte, record, time and queue limits. Local receipt does not prove an RF receive time or complete mesh coverage.

[Meshtastic device roles](https://meshtastic.org/docs/configuration/radio/device/), reviewed 2026-10-03, distinguishes routing/rebroadcast behavior; `CLIENT_MUTE` is not evidence that every RF transmission is disabled. [LoRa configuration](https://meshtastic.org/docs/configuration/radio/lora/), reviewed 2026-10-03, separately documents `tx_enabled` with a true default, modem parameters and region settings. A future receive-only adapter must require a previously prepared supported configuration and qualify its behavior. A local protocol write used to inspect configuration is distinct from an RF message, but its firmware effects must still be tested. Never silently change settings, request mesh responses or enable forwarding. Region selection guides configuration and does not establish operating permission. Generic LoRa and Meshtastic are separate protocol capabilities.

## Useful bounded receiver experiences

All external sources in this section were checked on 2026-10-03. These are selectable later experiences, not fixed live profiles, reception evidence or prerequisites for the first release. The shared [learning-experience design](../docs/design/learning-experiences.md) and [historical and modern learning research](43-historical-and-modern-learning.md) cover optional educational challenges; those activities do not grant receiver or transmit authority.

### People on a dial and signals crossing the world

A CB card can show channel, modulation, selected passage and a personal bookmark, with optional spectrum inspection. The [current US channel rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-95/subpart-D/section-95.963) lists forty centers between 26.965 and 27.405 MHz; channel numbering is not strictly frequency order. [Emission rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-95/subpart-D/section-95.971) distinguish AM, SSB and FM. This is a US technical reference, not a universal channel plan. Applicable jurisdiction, reception, persistence and publication need separately scoped profile gates. Opening a card never starts a scan, transcription or publication of conversations.

A shortwave propagation diary records an explicitly selected frequency, actual observation interval, antenna/settings, audible identification, fading and a bounded waterfall. [NIST's WWV page](https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/time-distribution/radio-station-wwv) supplies AM references at 2.5, 5, 10, 15 and 20 MHz, with 25 MHz explicitly experimental and interruptible. Hearing a time announcement does not calibrate the host clock: propagation, buffering and demodulation delay remain separate. [NOAA D-RAP](https://www.spaceweather.gov/content/global-d-region-absorption-prediction-documentation) models HF absorption. A separately requested dated overlay can support an explanation without becoming measured local reception or a prediction of every path.

The [NCDXF/IARU beacon network](https://www.ncdxf.org/beacon/intro.html) offers another later notebook: expected scheduled slot, candidate heard, confirmed callsign, not detected and not observed are distinct states. Its [schedule](https://www.ncdxf.org/beacon/) uses ten-second slots on five HF bands. Check actual status and clock uncertainty before a profile; schedule agreement alone does not identify a transmitter. A missed weak signal does not prove a propagation path closed. [HamSCI's WWV/H project](https://www.hamsci.org/wwv) provides a later reference-signal measurement direction, after receiver stability and clock qualification. These observations require no automatic upload or online schedule refresh.

### Owned sensors and relative spectrum

A known permitted weather sensor can become a small wireless science notebook: observation time, reported temperature, last report, gaps and expandable raw fields. [rtl_433](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433) documents many protocols and tested HackRF One through SoapySDR; that does not qualify the connected Pro, Windows build or Sigy adapter. Its [operation documentation](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433/blob/master/docs/OPERATION.md) supports offline samples and selected decoders. Prefer one known owned-device protocol with bounded local output. A checksum and repeated device ID do not authenticate a unique physical sensor. Do not implicitly enable all decoders, forwarding destinations or a neighboring-device inventory. Decoder and complete native/license graph selection remain open.

A relative spectrum notebook compares bounded selected-band observations under fixed antenna/gain/filter settings. Show relative dBFS, clipped-sample counts, observation duration and a disclosed FFT/window/normalization. These are not calibrated dBm, transmitter location, device identity or proof that one household device caused interference. Images, overload and unrelated emitters remain competing explanations. A later sweep must preserve dwell/retune timing and unobserved intervals; it cannot present sequential sampling as simultaneous whole-spectrum coverage.

### Aircraft maps and aviation voice

[FAA guidance](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/technology/equipadsb/resources/faq) distinguishes 1090ES and US 978 UAT. A first packet experience can replay one bounded 1090 observation and inspect frames before tracks. [VHF aviation voice](https://www.faa.gov/media/32956) occupies a separate AM communications band around 118 to 137 MHz. One Pro cannot continuously observe that voice band and 1090 MHz together; selecting either requires its own explicit tuning epoch. A second independently qualified receiver is the later alternative for simultaneous coverage. A packet map supplies no voice and is not a radar or navigation service.

Store raw frame ancestry, message type, parity/CRC checks, repaired bits and decoder revision. Positionless Mode S frames remain useful messages without invented coordinates. [Original CPR fixtures](https://github.com/wiedehopf/readsb/blob/dev/cprtests.c) distinguish airborne/surface and reference-dependent decoding. Require compatible frames, valid age/pairing and explicit reference assumptions; missing or ambiguous position stays unresolved. Add independent stale-pair, address mismatch, zone, date-line and malformed-frame fixtures before qualification. [Field definitions](https://github.com/wiedehopf/readsb/blob/dev/README-json.md) distinguish pressure/geometric altitude, reported integrity fields and last position versus last message age. Maps label received claimed positions, units and age; expired markers stop appearing current, while historical trails stay historical. One receiver does not establish multilateration. A claimed address/callsign or valid CRC does not authenticate identity or position, as [original ADS-B security research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3664) explains. Plausibility checks flag contradictions without proving authenticity or complete airspace coverage.

### Space passes and images

[ARISS's status page](https://www.ariss.org/current-status-of-iss-stations.html), checked today, reports a 437.800 MHz ISS voice-repeater downlink, 437.825 MHz APRS while troubleshooting, and an Oct 2 to Oct 6 SSTV event. These are volatile reported mission states, not reception promises or fixed Sigy presets; the event excerpt alone does not specify every required frequency/mode detail. Recheck a dated event notice before selecting a profile. Suitable antenna, measured link conditions and Doppler handling have separate gates. Saved orbital elements predict a pass, not physical detection. Automatic retuning requires an explicitly previewed finite plan, and no uplink belongs in the receive workflow.

The familiar NOAA-15/18/19 APT example is now archive-only: [NOAA's final retirement notice](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/legacy-orbit-noaa-decommissions-the-poes-satellite-constellation) confirms all three were decommissioned in 2025. [GOES HRIT/EMWIN](https://ospo.noaa.gov/operations/goes/hrit/index.html) is a current direct-broadcast candidate at 1694.1 MHz with approximately 1.205 MHz bandwidth. [NOAA mission information](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/index.php/our-satellites/currently-flying/geostationary-satellites) identifies GOES-18/19 as West/East; check [actual status](https://www.ospo.noaa.gov/operations/goes/status.html) before an experiment. Directional antenna, polarization, sky visibility, filter/LNA chain, link budget and packet/image decoding must be qualified independently. A downloaded weather image is network/archive data, never proof of local RF reception. Deep-space and astronomical signals need distinct sensitivity/aperture profiles; broad tuning capability is not a radio telescope. The [Strange Signals Desk](../docs/design/strange-signals.md) provides the archive-first path.

### An offline signal detective

Typed retained IQ can support a gentle inspect, compare hypotheses and reveal-reference flow, with audio only for a justified demodulation. The [rtl_433 regression collection](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433_tests) and public reference-signal projects are candidates for later independently labeled fixtures. Check each item's license, format, capture provenance and expected result before importing; no sample collection was downloaded or selected here. Authored puzzles remain authored or synthetic, independently of authentic recordings. Repeated processing is another transform over one observation. File replay to analysis or speakers grants no RF replay. Unknown signals remain observations and hypotheses without invented language or origin.

## Finite implementation and evidence gates

1. Preserve implemented ordered cached discovery while finishing focused source/recording inspection. Preserve applied query, selected identity, original scripts and truthful empty-cache state. This gives immediate utility without playback or hardware claims.
2. Implement the protected retained-range reader and real player lifecycle from DV-01. Measure native input/audio behavior, seek boundaries, retention exclusion, cancellation, generation races and background capture continuity. Then add explicit direct-live replacement; do not silently splice live connections into retained gaps.
3. Add typed file replay before device access: known IQ and packet fixtures with exact provenance, malformed/truncated input, unknown fields, clock gaps, clipping and bounded output. Choose an adapter boundary only after reviewing native distribution and license requirements. No implementation language expansion is needed.
4. Qualify one actual receive-only device profile at a time. Compare process-hosted tools, a narrowly reviewed native library and a general device abstraction against observed throughput, ownership and cancellation. Test RF silence through startup, traffic, reboot and reconnect with independent instrumentation. Require exact device/firmware/OS/backend receipts. Unverified autonomous transmit behavior refuses live attachment.

One tuner has one observed tuning epoch; scanning leaves unobserved intervals and cannot claim simultaneous coverage. Display decimation loses visual detail without dropping acquisition samples. Device disappearance, overflow and configuration drift create explicit gaps/holds, not fabricated silence. Unknown completion retains bounded protection until actual proof. These gates establish no current SDR/LoRa support, simultaneous capacity or release qualification.
