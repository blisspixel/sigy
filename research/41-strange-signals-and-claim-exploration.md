# Strange signals and claim exploration

Reviewed: 2026-10-03. Status: primary-source research and proposed future workflows. These proposals add no implemented SETI, IQ import, claim extraction, scientific qualification or autonomous investigation capability. This review fetched documentation only, without dataset downloads, native execution, paid requests or runtime edits.

The user requested an enjoyable, rigorous direction for exploring unusual subjects, including material encountered through Coast to Coast AM, Joe Rogan and other public broadcasts. This expresses a desired curiosity-driven experience, without assumptions about those audiences or endorsement of the claims discussed. The proposal follows [Intent](../INTENT.md), [signal extensions](../docs/planning/08-signal-extensions-and-workbench.md) and the [interpretation contract](../docs/design/signal-interpretation.md). Current radio, DVR, multilingual and bounded task work remains the release priority.

The project [blimpy repository](https://github.com/UCBerkeleySETI/blimpy) offers Python readers for filterbank, HDF5 and raw products. ## One coherent feature: the Strange Signals Desk

Use an optional investigation workspace with two typed entry points: an attributed broadcast claim and a measured signal observation. Both follow one journey: select an original, state a question, inspect context and alternatives, run an explicitly bounded comparison, and retain a replayable result. Claim text never becomes radio measurement evidence. An RF pattern never becomes a decoded message merely because a model can describe it fluently.

The appeal is hands-on exploration: a polished night-desk view, exact clips, a real waterfall when a supported dataset exists, linked chronology and a visible next experiment. Wonder can coexist with a useful conclusion such as an instrumental artifact, a repeated quotation or insufficient observations. Optional restrained copy could say "Interesting. Check the clock." or "The plot thickens. Keep the error bars." Humor belongs in navigation or learning hints, with a plain mode; never insert it into scientific result fields, machine output or sensitive personal material.

### Broadcast claim trail first

Open a retained passage and compare how a specific attributed claim appears across explicitly selected public radio and podcast material. Preserve original-script wording, transcript and translation revisions, speaker attribution status, exact recording and cue ranges, collection gaps and available playback. Recognition and speaker identity remain separate uncertainties. Users can choose qualified translation targets; English is a default, not a required pivot.

Keep at least four times distinct: original recording/media position, declared episode publication time, asserted event time and local observation/import time. A statement about a future event is not dated to its publication day. Unavailable or disputed dates retain their source and precision. Sort each lane by its declared time basis; never synthesize a precise chronological sequence from incomparable clocks.

Suggested relation vocabulary is quotation, paraphrase candidate, common cited source, explicit correction, disagreement and independent-observation claim. The last is an attributed statement until its provenance is checked. Two shows repeating one press release or interviewing the same witness do not become two independent measurements. Similar wording can reveal a lead, but cannot prove dependence or collusion. Different wording cannot prove independence. Corrections append history and show affected interpretations; they do not erase the earlier quote.

Show "earliest found in these 12 retained episodes" rather than "who said it first." Report searched source revisions, time bounds, language coverage, examined versus available material, truncation and missing retention. An empty literal search does not refute a proposition. A model-suggested contradiction must display both passages and the assumptions that make them incompatible; changed dates, referents, negation, irony and translations can alter the interpretation.

An initial package should let a person select two to eight existing citations, annotate a proposed relation and compare exact playback/context. It needs no universal fact classifier, truth score, live search agent or new graph database. Later extraction and relation suggestions must be separately evaluated and remain revisable.

### Replayable anomaly workbench later

Start with a selected public archival observation and its actual available product. A filterbank or HDF5 dynamic spectrum can support time-frequency exploration within its resolution. It cannot recover discarded phase or replace absent raw voltages. A raw IQ route requires a qualified numeric-format reader, sample clock, channels and tuning epochs. Unknown or incomplete metadata can support byte inspection, but must hold transforms that require missing information.

For every measurement or plot, retain original file identity and hash, upstream product/version/license, numeric representation, exact byte or sample range, time scale and uncertainty, frequency reference and units, sample rate, tuning changes, gaps, telescope/receiver metadata and available calibration. Save transform version and parameters, window and normalization, resolution, masks and the display reduction. A terminal waterfall needs axes, a legend and a visible decimation policy. Uncalibrated values should be called relative or arbitrary power where appropriate, without invented dBm or sky coordinates.

An observation checklist should ask whether instrument operation was checked, whether samples were missing or clipped, whether suitable on/off or simultaneous references exist, whether similar features occur at related frequencies, and whether a repeat observation has comparable sensitivity. Mark each item tested, unsupported, missing context or not attempted. Known-RFI labels need an attributed basis and applicability interval; a frequency allocation or absent database entry alone cannot identify or exclude a local emitter. Changing a display scale must not create a new detection result.

Keep detected feature, signal of interest, attributed explanation and unresolved interpretation separate. Record searched bandwidth, drift range, duration, thresholds and selection steps. Optional pattern ranking is specific to a measured profile and never an alien probability. Preserve competing terrestrial, instrumental and astronomical explanations without pretending every hypothesis is equally supported. Public astronomical data supports this workflow without receive hardware; a later HackRF profile is a different instrument and qualification problem.

### Explicit bounded watch

A watch follows selected source identities or selected archive metadata, inside a finite user grant. Its proposed manifest binds source revisions/destinations, recurrence or observation window, allowed transforms, maximum fetched bytes, retained bytes, CPU/work time, concurrent jobs, result count and zero paid allowance by default. Metadata changes or a new candidate cannot increase those bounds. No automatic archive mirror, telescope access, RF transmission or public announcement follows a finding.

Distinguish replaying the same retained bytes, reprocessing with a changed profile, discovering a new public file, and acquiring a physically new observation. Repeated processing is not independent redetection. If the archive contains no suitable later observation, report that limitation and the proposed distinguishing measurement. Do not claim that a local watch can instruct an observatory to perform it.

The service owns progress after client exit, exact admission, resumable work and cancellation. A long scan checkpoints finite input ranges and parameters, reports examined coverage and stopping reason, and publishes only from the current authorized generation. Partial results remain useful. Querying an earlier case does not silently dispatch reprocessing, refresh, downloads or providers.

## Small packages and acceptance evidence

1. **Existing-evidence comparison.** Design a citation-backed claim trail using retained audio and current immutable transcript/finding contracts. Render a compact two-passage comparison and a wider chronology. Accept fixtures for explicit corrections, one quotation repeated across sources, conflicting event dates, unknown speakers, original-script/translation disagreement, missing audio and bounded incomplete search. Existing task snapshots are evidence inputs, not semantic claim judgments.
2. **Typed archival import.** Select one modest licensed reference product and its parser contract, pin format/version and hash, and prove bounded header validation before allocation. Reject inconsistent lengths, byte-order mistakes, impossible sample rates and unsupported products without mutating the original. Independent numeric examples must detect swapped axes, reversed frequency direction, missing samples and time-origin errors. Data acquisition requires separate explicit authority.
3. **Observation replay.** Reproduce a published finite analysis or known interferer under declared settings. Compare an independent reference implementation or published measurements with tolerance and provenance. Include known artificial controls as clearly synthetic examples, mixed with real retained data only through explicit labels. No reference oracle may leak into a blinded detector's input.
4. **Finite watch and recovery.** Fault-test admission, interrupted import, cancellation, resource refusal, stale-generation publication, restart and retention. Measure one small-host and one desktop profile before claiming throughput. Test no-candidate, unavailable-reference and exhausted-budget states as carefully as successful feature detection.

Early success is a useful investigation with exact evidence, reproducible mechanics and clear alternatives. A false alarm understood well is a successful exploration outcome. Qualification of a detector requires declared searched classes, independently held-out cases, negative controls, measured false-positive and missed-detection behavior, selection effects and sensitivity. Neither a constructed demonstration nor a compelling BLC1 replay establishes performance on unknown signal families.

## Alternatives and risks

Prefer the existing archive/citation journey before an astronomy adapter. A general claim timeline can serve science, history, local news and ordinary broadcast comparisons. This avoids building a special truth engine for one genre. A later SETI preset should be a view and qualified transform profile over shared artifacts, not another scheduler, budget ledger or database.

Archive exploration offers reproducibility before hardware; live local SDR offers useful instrumental experiments but cannot inherit observatory sensitivity or celestial localization. A narrow dynamic-spectrum reader is smaller than full raw-voltage processing. SigMF is a candidate for sample metadata, while project-specific astronomy products retain their native documented schemas. Neither format should be forced onto incompatible data.

Risks include missing calibration or phase, source-clock errors, selection bias, multiple-comparison false alarms, model-made claim links, mistaken speaker attribution, duplicate sources presented as corroboration, and treating absence from a finite search as disproof. Include those limits at the relevant result, without burying them in a generic warning page. Avoid surveillance of private people, identity speculation, diagnostic labels, adversarial audience caricatures and jokes about distressing allegations. A playful instrument should help inspect a claim without endorsing unsupported accusations or rewarding escalating certainty.

For canonical planning, add this as future curiosity-led exploration after dependable radio/DVR/evidence foundations. Preserve the first-release scope. Require original evidence, explicit clock and unit basis, attributed claims, source-dependence uncertainty, replayable comparison, finite watch authority and optional plain presentation. No automatic scientific certification, discovery claim, unsupported social-conspiracy validation or universal decoder belongs in the product contract.
